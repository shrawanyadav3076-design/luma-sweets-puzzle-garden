How to Play

🍬 Match 3: Swap adjacent candies to match 3 identical candies in a row or column.

⚡ Striped Candies: Match 4 candies in a row. When matched, it clears the entire row or column with a laser blast!

🎁 Wrapped Candies: Match 5 in an L or T shape. Explodes in a 3x3 blast radius clearing all surrounding candies!

🌟 Rainbow Stars: Match 5 in a straight line. Swap it with any candy to clear every piece of that color from the board!

💥 Combos: Swap any two special pieces together for massive board-clearing reactions!

🔨 Boosters: Use Lollipop Hammers, Shuffles, Rainbow Bombs, and +5 Moves to conquer challenging levels.
