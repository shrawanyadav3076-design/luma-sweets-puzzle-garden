# PRIVACY POLICY FOR LUMA SWEETS: PUZZLE GARDEN

**Last Updated:** October 9, 2026

## 1. Introduction
Welcome to **Luma Sweets: Puzzle Garden** ("we", "our", or "the Game"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding data collection and usage when you play our game across Web, Android, and PWA platforms.

## 2. Information We Do NOT Collect
- We **do not** collect any personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email address, physical address, or phone number.
- We **do not** collect device identifiers (IMEI, Android ID, MAC address).
- We **do not** access or collect your device location (GPS or network location).
- We **do not** track you across external websites or services.

## 3. Local Data Storage
All game progress, including:
- Completed levels, star ratings, and high scores;
- Virtual sweet coin balances and booster counts;
- Audio, volume, haptic vibration, and reduced motion preferences;
- Daily login streak information;

is saved **strictly locally on your device** using browser `localStorage` or your app's local private sandboxed storage. This data is never transmitted to any external server or third-party cloud.

## 4. Third-Party Services & Advertising
- The game does **not** integrate third-party advertising SDKs or tracking cookies.
- No third-party analytics trackers are embedded in the game.

## 5. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K Compliance)
Because our game does not collect any personal data, it complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). Children of all ages can safely enjoy the game without risk of privacy invasion.

## 6. How to Delete Your Data
You can erase all locally stored game data at any time by navigating to:
**Settings > Reset All Progress** inside the game, or by clearing your browser's site data/cache for this application.
